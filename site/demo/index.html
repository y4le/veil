You are reading a decrypted page.

A moment ago this document was AES-256-GCM ciphertext. Your browser stretched the passphrase you typed, unwrapped the site key, decrypted the payload, and wrote the result into this tab; the server was never asked and never saw it.

Everything on this page was inside the ciphertext, including the title in your tab. Before you unlocked it, that title read “Protected page.”

Honesty

The plaintext of this page is committed to the public repository, so nothing here is secret. What is being demonstrated is that the deployed artifact really is ciphertext, and that the two zones really are independent.
